Make this password safer

A few changes that make patterns like the ones above much harder to guess:

  • Use a unique password for every account. Reused passwords mean one leak exposes everything else.
  • Use a password manager to generate and store long, random passwords you never have to memorize.
  • Prefer a passphrase — several unrelated words strung together — over a short word-plus-number pattern.
  • Turn on MFA or use passkeys wherever offered, so a guessed or leaked password alone isn't enough.
  • Avoid building passwords from public information — your name, birthday, jersey number, graduation year, pet's name, or team name are all things other people can find or guess.
